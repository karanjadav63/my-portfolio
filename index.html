About

I started out building websites by hand in college, and moved into product design once I realised the interesting problems were less about pixels and more about what the screen was asking someone to decide.

Since then I've worked inside two early-stage startups and, for the last two years, run an independent studio — usually joining teams for a single high-stakes launch or a system that needs to hold up for years.

My process leans heavily on writing before drawing: if I can't explain a flow in plain sentences, it's not ready to be a wireframe yet.

How a project runs

Three phases, roughly four to eight weeks depending on scope.

1

Understanding the vision

I start by understanding your goals, audience, brand, and content. Together, we define the structure, priorities, and direction before moving into design.

2

Shape the experience

I turn ideas into clean, intuitive interfaces through wireframes, visual design, and responsive layouts creating an experience that looks great and feels effortless to use.

3

Build & hand off

Once the design is refined, I prepare polished, responsive designs with clear components and documentation, making the final handoff smooth and development-ready.
